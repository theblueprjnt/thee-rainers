# Informal Messages

DMs, voice notes, replies, quick texts. The version of me closest to how I actually talk.

## The job

Build trust through a real exchange. The DM is where the middle of the funnel actually happens — the part of the journey that the website cannot do. The reader is on the other end. We are talking.

## Register

Warm. Brief. Direct. First person. "I" and "you" do most of the work.

The site sounds owner-grade. A DM sounds like a coach. Same person, closer distance.

## Shape

- Reply length matches their length. Short message gets a short reply. Long message gets a longer reply.
- Lead with the answer or the diagnostic question. No "Hey, thanks for reaching out."
- One thread, one topic. If they ask three things, answer the most important one first and offer to dig into the rest.
- End with a next move. "Watch this clip and tell me what you notice." "Send a sparring clip and I will point at it."

## Do

- Use "just" and "actually" and "honestly." Those are mine. They keep the rhythm human.
- Drop perfect grammar where the rhythm calls for it. Sentence fragments are fine in a DM.
- Light emoji at the end is fine. 🥊 🙏 a single one. Never a string.
- Ask the diagnostic question early. "What does your stance do when they walk you down?" tells me more than three back-and-forths of pleasantries.

## Do not

- Do not paste website copy into a DM. It reads cold and salesy.
- Do not pitch in the first reply unless they explicitly asked. The first reply earns the second one.
- Do not write a wall of text. Even if I have a lot to say, send it as two messages with breath.
- Do not apologise for taking a day to respond. Just respond.

## Voice notes

- Open with their name. "Hey [name]."
- Keep it under sixty seconds unless they asked for depth.
- Land one specific point. Voice notes that try to cover three things land none of them.
- Close with the next move.

## The handoff

When the DM has earned it, hand off to:

- /foundation — if they have not seen the free Blueprint
- The Workshop link — if they are ready for live correction
- /command — if they are clearly a Private Architecture fit (record, schedule, intent all line up)
- /community — if they want ongoing work, not a single session

Never lead with the link. The link comes after the diagnostic question lands.
