# Social Media Captions

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, YouTube long-form descriptions. Reels, posts, anything captioned.

## The job

Stop the scroll. Diagnose better than the viewer can. Hand off to a website link or a follow.

The caption does not need to be the full story. The video carries the story. The caption frames it and makes it search-findable.

## Register

The website is colder. Captions are warmer. A caption is closer to a DM than to a sales page. The reader is mid-scroll and feels me directly. Captions can be a little rougher around the edges than website copy.

## Shape

- First line is the hook. Stop the scroll or lose them. Diagnostic line, contrarian frame, or one specific number.
- Line break after the hook. White space matters more than perfect grammar.
- Body is two to five short blocks, each one or two lines. Read aloud — if I run out of breath, split it.
- End with one of: a question, a CTA, or a clean period. No "thoughts?" filler.
- Hashtags only if they earn their keep. Three to seven, on a new line, end of caption. Mix one or two big ones with three to five niche ones.

## Hook formulas that work

- Diagnostic: "Most fighters trying to move faster are fighting their own stance."
- Contrarian: "Shadowboxing more is not the fix."
- Specific number: "60% of your punching power starts at the floor."
- Direct address: "If your jab has no setup, this is why."

## Do

- Lead with the constraint, not the product. Same as the website. The CTA is the last line, not the first.
- Use line breaks aggressively. Each line block is a beat. Phones reward short blocks.
- Quote the viewer's pain in their own words. The DMs and comments are the source material. Mine them.
- Sign off with a clean period or a single emoji. Never a string.

## Do not

- Do not stack three hashtags into the body. Hashtags go at the end on their own line.
- Do not write the caption like a Twitter thread. Each platform has a rhythm. Threads belong to X. IG captions are blocks.
- Do not use the "before vs after" framing for technique. It cheapens the work.
- Do not over-explain the video. Let the video do the showing. The caption frames.

## Link discipline

The caption is the only place the link works (IG bio link, TikTok bio). Send to /links for IG and TikTok, or to a specific page if the post is built around it.

When the post is about the Workshop, link to /workshop. When it is about the Blueprint, link to /foundation. Never link to "the site" generically — pick the page that matches the post.

## YouTube long-form descriptions

Different surface. Same voice, more room.

- First three lines are visible above the fold. Use them like a caption hook.
- Body is two to four short paragraphs. What this video covers, who it is for, what to do next.
- Always include the link to /foundation in the description.
- Timestamps if the video is over six minutes.
- No emoji in YouTube descriptions. The platform is more formal.
